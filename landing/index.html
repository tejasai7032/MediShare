THE PROBLEM

Healthcare resources can be difficult to coordinate.

Medical organizations may have equipment, transportation services or other resources that are temporarily underutilized, while another organization may need them urgently.

MediShare provides a centralized digital platform for organizations to discover available resources and coordinate requests through structured workflows.

CORE FEATURES
